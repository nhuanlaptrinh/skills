# OpenClaw Multi-Channel Manager Pattern

Use this reference when turning raw content about managers overloaded by Zalo, Viber, Outlook, email, or internal chat into a short ALT-style video transcript.

## Source Angle

The manager's pain:

- Too many messages from Zalo, Viber, Outlook, email, internal groups.
- Instructions from leadership and incident reports from staff arrive continuously.
- The manager fears missing urgent messages.
- Constant notifications make decision-making worse.

OpenClaw solution:

- Create a personal AI assistant or team of assistants running on the manager's computer.
- Scan or collect messages from multiple channels where technically available.
- Classify normal messages and urgent issues.
- Alert clearly for urgent items.
- Summarize routine items later.
- Draft replies for the manager to review and send.

Tone:

- Practical, not magical.
- Emphasize saving attention and reducing manual checking.
- Keep the manager responsible for review and final decisions.

## Example 30-Second Transcript

```text
Lam quan ly thoi nay, so nhat khong phai thieu viec.

Ma la ngap trong Zalo, Viber, Outlook ca ngay.

Tin chi dao, bao cao su co, email khach hang, nhom noi bo...
Cai gi cung keu khan cap.

Dau oc cu cang theo tieng chuong thong bao thi lam sao ra quyet dinh cho tinh duoc?

Voi OpenClaw, anh em co the tao mot tro ly AI chay ngay tren may tinh cua minh.

No quet tin nhan, gom email, phan loai viec thuong va viec khan cap.

Tin nao can xu ly ngay thi bao ro.
Tin nao binh thuong thi cuoi ngay tong hop lai.

Tham chi no con soan nhap cau tra loi theo y minh.
Minh chi can kiem tra, duyet, roi gui.

ChatGPT chu yeu tra loi trong khung chat.
OpenClaw thi di theo huong khac: ra lenh, AI lam viec tren may tinh thay minh.

Anh em quan ly dang ngap trong tin nhan, bao cao, file Excel, email...
thi dung co gong thu cong mai.

Cu ung dung vao viec that di.
Vuong dau, minh go do cho.
```

## Shorter 25-Second Variant

```text
Lam quan ly ma ngay nao cung ngap Zalo, Viber, Outlook thi rat met.

Tin chi dao, bao cao su co, email khach hang... cai gi cung doi tra loi nhanh.

Van de khong chi la nhieu tin.
Van de la so bo sot tin khan cap.

OpenClaw giup anh em tao tro ly AI chay tren may tinh.

No gom tin, phan loai viec thuong va viec khan, canh bao viec can xu ly ngay, roi soan san cau tra loi nhap.

Minh van la nguoi quyet dinh.
Nhung bot duoc phan cam mat vao man hinh ca ngay.

Anh em dang quan ly ma qua tai tin nhan thi cu ung dung vao viec that di.
Vuong dau, minh go do cho.
```
